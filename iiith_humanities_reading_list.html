Exact Humanities Reading List

An incomplete list of reading material and course syllabus material from the IIIT-Hyderabad's short-lived Exact Humanities Dual Degree Master's program.

Notable holes

  • Most of the classical Sanskrit original sources, commentaries, and excerpts. The grainy PDFs have mostly not survived my various computer migrations.
  • Course lists from the syllabus, and the mapping from reading to course.
  • Notes on each reading, mine and the lecturer's both.
  • Original context of presentation. Each course was very opinionated – several of our professors and most notably Prof. Navjyoti were using the classroom as a setting to further develop their own original thought – so secondary material misses out on a lot of what was going on.
  • Who cared about what. Each lecturer had a notable focus, opinion, and supporting material. This mishmash is unrepresentative of the variety.
